Points 101

How I go from swiping a card to boarding a plane.

  1. Earn

    Pick a card or two whose bonus categories match what you already spend on, like groceries, dining or travel. Never carry a balance; interest wipes out the value of the points.

  2. Transfer

    Flexible bank points can move to many airline and hotel partners. Only transfer once you've found award space, because transfers usually can't be undone.

  3. Book

    Search award availability early or right before departure, when airlines often release seats. Be flexible on dates and the deals show up.

  4. Fly

    Enjoy the lounge. Then start planning the next one.
